Deterministic OpenAI and Anthropic compatible router for the [make.fun free-model-aggregator bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/free-model-aggregator-01). It discovers the sealed provider directory, reports the exact catalog, ranks legal free models by rating and capabilities, fails over on provider refusals, and adapts OpenAI, Anthropic, and Gemini wire formats.
